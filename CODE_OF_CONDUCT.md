# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inspiring, and harassment-free environment for all contributors and community members, regardless of:

- Age
- Body size
- Caste
- Disability
- Ethnicity
- Gender identity and expression
- Level of experience
- Nationality
- Personal appearance
- Political belief
- Race
- Religion
- Sex
- Sexual identity and orientation
- Socioeconomic status
- Tribal affiliation

We pledge to create and maintain a community where **everyone feels safe, valued, and able to contribute their best work**.

---

## Our Standards

### Positive Behaviors

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

✅ **Inclusivity & Respect**

- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Welcome newcomers and provide constructive feedback

✅ **Professionalism**

- Give and accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on the code and ideas, not the person
- Provide clear, helpful communication

✅ **Collaboration**

- Help others learn and grow
- Share knowledge and resources
- Celebrate others' contributions and successes

✅ **Accountability**

- Take responsibility for your mistakes
- Work to improve the community
- Address conflicts directly and professionally

### Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are unacceptable and will not be tolerated:

❌ **Harassment & Discrimination**

- Harassment of any kind (including sexual harassment)
- Discriminatory jokes, language, or imagery
- Unwelcome sexual advances or attention
- Intimidation or threats

❌ **Abusive Communication**

- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Deliberately spreading misinformation
- Spam or flooding channels
- Excessive self-promotion

❌ **Disruptive Behavior**

- Deliberate disruption of discussions
- Off-topic arguments or derailing conversations
- Deliberate attempts to marginalize or exclude people
- Persistent behavior violations after warning

❌ **Privacy Violations**

- Sharing others' personal information without consent
- Publishing private communications without permission
- Doxxing or targeting individuals

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it:

1. **Report to Maintainers**: Contact project maintainers directly
   - Email: [maintainer-email]
   - GitHub: Private message to maintainers

2. **Report on GitHub**: Use the "Report abuse" feature for:
   - Issues
   - Pull requests
   - Comments
   - Discussions

3. **Report Anonymously**: Use this form [link if available]

### What to Include

When reporting, provide:

- Description of the incident
- Who was involved
- When and where it occurred
- Any supporting evidence (screenshots, links)
- Impact and concerns

**All reports will be treated confidentially and investigated promptly.**

### Investigation & Response

The project maintainers will:

1. **Acknowledge** your report within 48 hours
2. **Investigate** the incident thoroughly
3. **Communicate** the outcome to relevant parties
4. **Take action** as appropriate

### Consequences

Unacceptable behavior from any community member will result in:

**First Offense:**

- Warning and explanation of violation
- Request to cease the behavior

**Repeated Violations:**

- Temporary suspension from participation
- Time-limited removal from channels/repositories

**Severe or Persistent Violations:**

- Permanent ban from the community
- Removal from all project spaces

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- ✅ All project spaces (GitHub, issues, pull requests, discussions)
- ✅ Project-related external spaces (forums, social media, etc.)
- ✅ Project events and conferences
- ✅ Any space where community members represent the project

**Note:** Community members may choose to address violations that occur outside project spaces if they substantially affect project safety and community wellbeing.

## Inclusivity Guidelines

### Language

- Use inclusive, non-gendered language ("they/them" instead of "he/she")
- Avoid idioms and cultural references that may be exclusionary
- Be mindful of accessibility in communication

### Accessibility

- Describe images and provide alt-text for screenshots
- Use clear, simple language
- Provide transcripts for videos or audio
- Structure discussions for screen readers

### Diversity

- Welcome perspectives from diverse backgrounds
- Recognize that there are multiple ways to solve problems
- Learn from different approaches and experiences
- Actively seek input from underrepresented groups

## Support & Resources

### For Contributors

If you're experiencing issues or need support:

- Reach out to project maintainers
- Connect with other community members
- Seek mentorship for skill development
- Share knowledge and help others learn

### Conflict Resolution

For conflicts between community members:

1. **Talk directly** with the other person if comfortable
2. **Involve a mediator** (project maintainer) if needed
3. **Follow formal reporting** if serious
4. **Focus on resolution** rather than blame

## Questions & Clarification

If you have questions about this Code of Conduct:

1. **Check the FAQ** section below
2. **Ask in discussions** or open an issue
3. **Contact maintainers** for clarification

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What if I made a mistake?**
A: We're all learning. If you make a mistake, apologize sincerely, learn from it, and move forward. We value growth and genuine effort to improve.

**Q: Can I report anonymously?**
A: Yes. We respect privacy and will investigate anonymous reports professionally. However, we may need more details to properly address the issue.

**Q: What if I'm accused of a violation?**
A: You'll be given the opportunity to respond. We investigate fairly and ensure both parties are heard before taking action.

**Q: Does this apply to private conversations?**
A: While we can't monitor private messages, reported violations in private conversations may still be investigated if they significantly impact community safety.

**Q: What about disagreements about technical decisions?**
A: Technical disagreements are healthy! This Code of Conduct addresses _how_ we have those discussions, not whether we should have them. Be respectful and constructive.
